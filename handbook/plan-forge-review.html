Plan · Forge · Review

Told from the actors' side — who speaks, to whom, and in what order, as a ticket becomes a merged change.

Every line below is a comment on the ticket thread or a note on the PR/MR — the thread is the whole record, no side channels. The bracketed tokens are the gates; their grammar is the table at the foot.

The cast

  • Elrond the human — opens the ticket, summons, and renders every verdict.
  • Erestor the council (/council, /glorfindel) — drafts and refines the plan.
  • Celebrimbor the forge (/celebrimbor, /aule) — branches, smiths, opens the PR, closes it on merge.
  • Mithrandir the review (/mithrandir, /lindir, /nazgul) — weighs the PR and renders a verdict.
  • Narvi the amends (/narvi, /durin) — answers each comment, one commit at a time.

The longer road — skeleton stage

For work too large to plan in one leap, a skeleton is carved before the forge. Two [FORTH]s gate the arc — one to approve the plan, one to approve the skeleton. 

graph TD
    A["Elrond — [MELLON]
summons into the skeleton sweep"] --> B["Erestor — [PLAN]
the living plan, edited in place"]
    B --> V1{"Elrond's verdict"}
    V1 -->|FORTH · plan| S["Celebrimbor — [SKELETON]
stubs + diagram PNG"]
    S --> V2{"Elrond's verdict"}
    V2 -->|FORTH · skeleton| G["Celebrimbor — [GWAITH]
the code arrives as a draft PR"]

/aule and /glorfindel drive these rungs under /loop: each tick derives a ticket's rung from its thread via hooks/skeleton-rung.py and dispatches the plan, skeleton, or forge work accordingly.

The comment grammar

Seven tokens carry state; everything else is counsel. Matching is case-insensitive; English aliases are recognized everywhere their Tolkien primaries are.

Token Alias Author Meaning
[COUNSEL vN] [PLAN vN] Erestor Draft of the plan; N increments each round
[PARLEY] [AGENT-ASK] Erestor A single clarifying question — speech to come to terms
[MELLON] [FRIEND] Elrond Summons — enrols a planless ticket in the skeleton-stage sweeps
[FORTH] [APPROVE] Elrond The plan stands; council adjourns with approval
[NAY] [REJECT] Elrond The plan is abandoned; council adjourns without approval
[NAMARIE] [FAREWELL] Elrond Farewell — adjourn without verdict
[GWAITH] [FORGED], [SHIPPED] Celebrimbor The deed is wrought; PR/MR opened, body carries the URL

Verdict precedence is by token, not by chronology: [FORTH] > [NAY] > [NAMARIE]. Safe to overrule yourself in the same thread.

Run whole — /anduin and the watch

The arc above is a stage at a time, run by hand. Two skills run it whole.

/anduin <tracker> <project> rides the pipeline in one pass — /glorfindel for the plan stage, then /aule for the forge stage, sequential by construction so the two never contend. It prints one combined report closing in a plain STIRRED / QUIET verdict. The forge runs unattended by default (--auto --max 3) — there is no --auto to type; pass --dry-run (forge nothing) or --confirm (prompt per PR) to gate it.

/amon-sul — Amon Sûl, the Watch — wraps a sweep in an adaptive, in-session loop. Name the rider and it rides: /amon-sul anduin youtrack URD --filter "#Unresolved" watches the whole pipeline. Each tick it runs one pass, reads the road, and self-schedules the next ride — tightening when work stirs, stretching as the road stays quiet.

The cadence answers the road, not the clock — a named back-off ladder, capped at one hour (the ScheduleWakeup ceiling):

Streak (quiet rides) Next ride in
0 — the road stirred 5 min
1 10 min
2 20 min
3 30 min
≥ 4 1 hr — the ceiling

An optional working-hours window (--active 09-18, or a per-project working_hours.md default) holds the watch through the dark: an off-hours wake skips the ride whole — no sweep, no forge — and the streak freezes until the window next opens. The watch is session-bound: the vigil dies when the session closes. Say “stop the vigil” to end it. For a truly silent night on a long-dead board, reach for a durable cron instead — this watch only hops the hours, it does not sleep them.
