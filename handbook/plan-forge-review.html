Plan · Forge · Review

Told from the actors' side — who speaks, to whom, and in what order, as a ticket becomes a merged change.

Every line below is a comment on the ticket thread or a note on the PR/MR — the thread is the whole record, no side channels. The bracketed tokens are the gates; their grammar is the table at the foot.

The cast

  • Elrond the human — opens the ticket, summons, and renders every verdict.
  • Erestor the council (/council, /glorfindel) — drafts and refines the plan.
  • Celebrimbor the forge (/celebrimbor) — branches, smiths, and opens the PR.
  • Aulë the smith-lord (/aule) — sweeps a project's approved counsels into the forge, and closes each ticket with [METTA] once its PR merges.
  • Mithrandir the review (/mithrandir) — weighs whether the code is good, and renders Merge / Hold / Refuse.
  • Mandos the doom (/mandos) — weighs whether it is the right code: Covered / Missing / Scope-crept against the ticket's own goal.
  • Narvi the amends (/narvi, /durin, /moria) — answers each comment, one commit at a time; Durin sweeps a repo, Moria every repo on the mend list.
  • The gate-keepers — /argonath weighs the diff before it is pushed, /celebrant merges and sweeps the branch away, and /working is the other door into the forge, cutting a branch straight from a ticket.

Two more stand beside the review but off this road: /nazgul weighs a local diff or a standing tree — it takes no PR URL — and /lindir renders a reviewer's brief rather than a verdict.

The three diagrams below are rendered in the browser by mermaid, fetched from a CDN — the one thing in this handbook that needs the network. Offline they show their source instead, which still reads.

The longer road — YouTrack

A skeleton is carved before the forge, and two [FORTH]s gate the arc — one to approve the plan, one to approve the skeleton. The rung is chosen by the tracker, never by the size of the work: YouTrack always rides it, Jira never can. 

graph TD
    A["Elrond — [MELLON]
summons into the skeleton sweep"] --> B["Erestor — [PLAN]
the living plan, edited in place"]
    B --> V1{"Elrond's verdict"}
    V1 -->|FORTH · plan| S["Celebrimbor — [SKELETON]

    S --> V2{"Elrond's verdict"}
    V2 -->|FORTH · skeleton| G["Celebrimbor — [GWAITH]
the code arrives as a draft PR"]

/glorfindel drives the plan rung; /aule drives the skeleton and the forge. Each derives a ticket's rung from its thread via hooks/skeleton-rung.py and dispatches accordingly. To keep them riding, wrap them in /amon-sul, the adaptive watch that replaced the older /loop pass.

The comment grammar

Fourteen tokens carry state; everything else is counsel. Matching is case-insensitive; English aliases are recognized everywhere their Tolkien primaries are. The canon is skills/council/SKILL.md, Comment grammar — this table follows it.

Token Alias Author Meaning
[COUNSEL vN] [PLAN vN] Erestor Draft of the plan; N increments each round
[PARLEY] [AGENT-ASK] Erestor A single clarifying question — speech to come to terms
[PEDO] [ANSWER] Erestor / the smith Speak, and answer. The reply to a [CEIST] or to bare prose — the plan stands untouched. Uncounted toward the turn limit
[VINYA] [RENEWED] Council Renewed. Appended after the living plan comment is edited in place, since a silent edit fires no notification
[ENWINA] [STALE] Council Old — of the elder days. Posted once at approval when the accepted plan has outgrown the ticket description. Names the drift; never rewrites it
[MELLON] [FRIEND] Elrond Summons — enrols a planless ticket in the skeleton-stage sweeps. Single-ticket /council ignores it; the invocation is itself consent
[CEIST] [ASK] Elrond A question put to the council. Draws a [PEDO]; the plan is untouched. Bare prose reads the same way
[ENVINYA] [ALTER] Elrond Renew it. The one human word that redrafts the standing plan or skeleton. Absent it, fresh prose only draws an answer
[FORTH] [APPROVE] Elrond The plan stands; council adjourns with approval
[NAY] [REJECT] Elrond The plan is abandoned; council adjourns without approval
[NAMARIE] [FAREWELL] Elrond Farewell — adjourn without verdict
[GWAITH] [FORGED], [SHIPPED] Celebrimbor The deed is wrought; PR/MR opened, body carries the URL. Council never posts it; /celebrimbor does
[DOOM] [VERDICT] Mandos The Doom. Whether the deed matches the decree — Covered / Missing / Scope-crept. Never redrafts or approves; posted by /mandos post
[METTA] — Aulë The end. Closed on merge — the ticket moved to the project's closed state. Terminal; posted by /aule, never by council
[PLAN] — Council The living plan on the skeleton road — one comment, edited in place, carrying a <!-- consumed: … --> watermark. Distinct from the versioned [PLAN vN] alias above
[SKELETON] — Celebrimbor The living skeleton — file tree and stubbed signatures — one comment, edited in place

Verdict precedence is by token, not by chronology: [FORTH] > [NAY] > [NAMARIE]. Safe to overrule yourself in the same thread.

Run whole — /anduin and the watch

The arc above is a stage at a time, run by hand. Three skills run it whole: /anduin over one project, /rhovanion over every project in the pipeline list, and /amon-sul standing watch over either.

/anduin <tracker> <project> rides the pipeline in one pass — /glorfindel for the plan stage, then /aule for the forge stage, sequential by construction so the two never contend. It prints one combined report closing in a plain STIRRED / QUIET verdict. The forge runs unattended by default (--auto --max 3) — there is no --auto to type; pass --dry-run (forge nothing) or --confirm (prompt per PR) to gate it.

/rhovanion widens that to every project in the pipeline list — but gates each behind a cheap movement probe, so a quiet project costs one tracker query rather than a whole pipeline. It rides projects sequentially, so no two contend, and closes in the same STIRRED / QUIET verdict.

/amon-sul — Amon Sûl, the Watch — wraps a sweep in an adaptive, in-session loop. Name the rider and it rides: /amon-sul anduin youtrack URD --filter "#Unresolved" watches the whole pipeline. Each tick it runs one pass, reads the road, and self-schedules the next ride — tightening when work stirs, stretching as the road stays quiet.

    Q -->|STIRRED| T["streak = 0

    Q -->|QUIET| C["streak + 1

    style O fill:#f6e7d6,stroke:#9a3b2e

The cadence answers the road, not the clock — a named back-off ladder, capped at one hour (the ScheduleWakeup ceiling):

Streak (quiet rides) Next ride in
0 — the road stirred 5 min
1 10 min
2 20 min
3 30 min
≥ 4 1 hr — the ceiling

An optional working-hours window (--active 09-18, or a per-project working_hours.md default) holds the watch through the dark: an off-hours wake skips the ride whole — no sweep, no forge — and the streak freezes until the window next opens. The watch is session-bound: the vigil dies when the session closes. Say “stop the vigil” to end it. For a truly silent night on a long-dead board, reach for a durable cron instead — this watch only hops the hours, it does not sleep them.
